01 // THE FACILITY
ORION RESEARCH FACILITY
The Orion Research Facility is a restricted, highly secure research center operated by Helix Dynamics.
Deep within its walls lies Project 9, an advanced artificial intelligence system designed to predict human behavior and anticipate future events with terrifying accuracy.
Until tonight, it was Helix Dynamics' most prized, closely guarded secret.

02 // THE INCIDENT
THE LOCKED ROOM
At approximately 02:19 AM, Dr. Vikram Sen, the director of the facility, was found dead inside his private laboratory.
The laboratory was securely locked from the inside. There were no signs of forced entry, no struggle, and no immediate cause of death apparent to the first responders.
The room appeared impenetrable. Yet, the facility's access logs, surveillance records, and system timestamps tell a conflicting story. They do not agree with each other, or with the statements of the staff.

03 // THE ANOMALY
THE EIGHTH SIGNATURE
A suspicious discrepancy has been uncovered in the facility's internal sensor records: eight human signatures were detected in the restricted sector tonight.
However, only seven authorized personnel were recorded as being present in the facility.
Someone manipulated the system. Someone inside the facility knows far more than they are admitting.

04 // THE PREDICTION
PROJECT 9
Before Vikram Sen's body was discovered, Project 9 had already generated a prediction connected to his death.
Your objective is to uncover what happened, who caused it, why it happened, and how Project 9 became involved in the director's demise.
Trust no one's initial statement. The evidence will not lie.
